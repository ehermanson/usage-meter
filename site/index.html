  • Reads what you already have. Uses your local Claude Code, Codex CLI, and Gemini sign-ins. No account, no API keys to paste.
  • Stays out of the way. Lives in the menu bar. The title shows your highest 5-hour usage; open it for every window and reset time.
  • Warns before you're cut off. Bars turn yellow at 75% and red at 90%.

Everything stays on your Mac. Usage Meter reads local credentials, talks only to each provider's own usage endpoint, and sends nothing anywhere else. No analytics, no telemetry.

Download the DMG → drag to Applications → it appears in your menu bar.
